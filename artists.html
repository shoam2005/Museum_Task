Artists

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent Willem van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch Post-Impressionist painter who became one of the most influential figures in Western art history. Known for his bold colors, dramatic brushstrokes, and emotional depth, he created over 2,000 artworks in just a decade—including iconic pieces like *The Starry Night* and *Sunflowers*. Despite struggling with mental illness and selling only one painting during his lifetime, today he is recognized as a master of modern art.

Claude Monet

Oscar-Claude Monet (1840–1926) was a French painter and a key founder of Impressionism, a movement that transformed Western art by capturing light, color, and fleeting moments. Renowned for painting outdoors (*en plein air*), he created masterworks like *Impression, Sunrise*—which gave the movement its name—and his iconic series of *Water Lilies*. Today, he is celebrated as one of the most influence-defining figures in modern art history.

Rembrandt van Rijn

Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669) was a Dutch Golden Age painter and printmaker, widely considered one of the greatest visual artists in history. Renowned for his mastery of light and shadow, emotional realism, and expressive self-portraits, he created timeless masterpieces like *The Night Watch*..
